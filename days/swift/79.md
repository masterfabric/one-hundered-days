# Day 79: UI Testing & Accessibility - Preview-Driven Verification

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Preview Multiple States:** Add previews for loading, empty, error, and populated states.
2.  **Preview Accessibility:** Preview at large Dynamic Type and in dark mode.
3.  **Use Preview Data:** Feed views deterministic sample data.
4.  **Catch Regressions Early:** Scan previews as a fast visual check before building.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Add a preview per meaningful state, not just one showing the happy path. Loading, empty, error, and populated — four `#Preview` blocks, each passing a view model or sample data in that state. You now have a visual test matrix you can scan in seconds, and the empty and error states stop being things you discover in production because they were never once rendered during development.

Then preview the accessibility variants. `.environment(\.dynamicTypeSize, .accessibility3)` and `.preferredColorScheme(.dark)` on additional previews catch clipped text and invisible-on-dark colours immediately. These two overrides find most of what Day 78 warns about, without leaving the canvas.

Feed them deterministic sample data. A `static let sample` on your model, or a small fixtures file, keeps previews stable and makes them read as documentation of what the view expects. Anything random, network-backed, or date-dependent makes previews flicker and eventually get ignored — and a preview nobody trusts is a preview nobody looks at.

Finally, make scanning them a habit before you build. Previews give you much of the value of snapshot testing at almost no setup cost, and the tighter the loop between a change and seeing its effect, the more design problems you catch while they're still cheap to fix. Views written to be previewable — small, taking their data as inputs — are the same views that are easy to test and reuse.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

By the end of today, you will use rich, multi-state previews as a fast visual test matrix, catching layout and accessibility regressions before building.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Preview Matrix:** A set of previews covering a view's meaningful states.
*   **Sample Data:** Deterministic fixture data used to drive previews.
*   **Visual Regression:** An unintended change in a view's appearance.
*   **Feedback Loop:** The cycle time between making a change and seeing its effect.
*   **Fixture:** A fixed piece of sample data used to drive previews and tests deterministically.
