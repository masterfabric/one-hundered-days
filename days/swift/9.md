# Day 9: Types & Protocols - Protocols & Conformance

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Declare a Protocol:** Define a protocol with required properties and methods.
2.  **Conform a Type:** Make a `struct` conform and implement the requirements.
3.  **Use Protocol as a Type:** Accept `any Protocol` as a parameter (existential).
4.  **Adopt Standard Protocols:** Conform to `Equatable`, `Hashable`, and `Comparable`.

## 📖 Task Explanations

A protocol is a contract: it declares what a conforming type must provide without dictating how. Protocols are the backbone of abstraction in Swift — you program against capabilities (`Equatable`, `Identifiable`, `Codable`) rather than concrete types, which keeps code flexible and testable. Standard-library protocols unlock huge amounts of behavior for free: conform to `Hashable` and your type works in a `Set` or as a dictionary key; conform to `Comparable` and you can sort. Understanding conformance is the gateway to protocol-oriented programming, generics, and clean architecture.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will define contracts with protocols and conform your types to them, unlocking abstraction and the free behavior that standard protocols provide.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Protocol:** A contract specifying properties and methods a type must implement.
*   **Conformance:** A type fulfilling a protocol's requirements.
*   **Existential (`any`):** A boxed value known only by the protocol it conforms to.
*   **`Equatable`/`Hashable`:** Standard protocols enabling equality checks and hashing.
