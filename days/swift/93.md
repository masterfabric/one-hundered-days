# Day 93: Platform Integration - App Intents & Widgets

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Define an `AppIntent`:** Expose an app action to Siri, Shortcuts, and Spotlight.
2.  **Build a Widget:** Create a home-screen widget with WidgetKit and a timeline.
3.  **Add Interactivity:** Make a widget button trigger an App Intent.
4.  **Provide Entities:** Expose your data to the system via App Intents entities.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Define an `AppIntent`: a struct with a title, a `perform()` method, and any parameters, and your app's action becomes something the system can invoke — from Siri, the Shortcuts app, Spotlight, or a widget. Design each intent around one user-meaningful action rather than exposing your internal API surface.

Then build a widget with WidgetKit. A widget is a separate extension target with its own limited runtime: it isn't a live view of your app, it renders a *timeline* of entries you supply, and the system decides when to display and refresh them. That timeline model is the main mental adjustment — you're providing content for the next few hours, not responding to the moment.

Add interactivity by putting a `Button(intent:)` in the widget. Tapping runs your App Intent in the background and refreshes the timeline without opening the app, which is what makes "mark as done" or "start timer" work directly from the home screen. Keep those intents fast and side-effect-light, since they run under tight constraints.

Finally, expose your data as App Intent entities so the system can reference specific items — a note, a task, a workout — in shortcuts and Siri suggestions. This whole surface is increasingly where users encounter an app: on the home screen, in search, through Siri. Treating it as an afterthought means the app is only ever found by someone deliberately opening it.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

By the end of today, you will expose app functionality to the system with App Intents and build interactive widgets with WidgetKit.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **App Intent:** A defined action the system can invoke (Siri/Shortcuts/widgets).
*   **WidgetKit:** The framework for building home/Lock Screen widgets.
*   **Timeline:** The schedule of entries driving a widget's content.
*   **Entity:** App data exposed to the system via App Intents.
*   **Widget Extension:** A separate target with a limited runtime that renders widget timelines.
