# Day 67: Animations - Transitions

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Add Insertion/Removal Transitions:** Apply `.transition` to views entering and leaving.
2.  **Combine Transitions:** Compose transitions with `.combined(with:)`.
3.  **Asymmetric Transitions:** Use different insert and remove effects.
4.  **Animate Conditional Content:** Wrap conditional views so transitions trigger.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Transitions animate a view entering or leaving the hierarchy, which is a different problem from animating a property. `.transition(.slide)` on a conditionally-shown view describes how it should arrive and depart, replacing the abrupt pop that an unanimated insert produces.

Compose them for richer effects. `.opacity.combined(with: .scale)` fades and grows at once, and `.move(edge: .bottom).combined(with: .opacity)` gives you the standard sheet-like entrance. Composition beats writing a custom transition for almost everything you'll want.

Asymmetric transitions let arrival and departure differ: `.asymmetric(insertion: .move(edge: .trailing), removal: .opacity)`. This matches how people read motion — a new item sliding in from the side, a deleted one simply fading — and using the same effect in both directions often looks wrong even when it's technically symmetric.

Finally, the part that trips everyone up: a transition only runs if the change is animated *and* the view's identity actually changes. So wrap the state change in `withAnimation`, and make sure the conditional produces a genuine insert or remove rather than a modified view — an `if` inside a container usually works, while a ternary that swaps a property does not. When a transition mysteriously doesn't fire, identity is nearly always the reason.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

By the end of today, you will animate views entering and leaving with composed, asymmetric transitions, smoothing every appearance and dismissal.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Transition:** An animation for a view's insertion or removal.
*   **Combined Transition:** Two transitions merged into one effect.
*   **Asymmetric Transition:** Different effects for insertion versus removal.
*   **View Identity:** The property change that triggers a transition to run.
*   **Insertion/Removal:** The two events a transition animates as a view joins or leaves the tree.
