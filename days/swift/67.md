# Day 67: Animations - Transitions

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Add Insertion/Removal Transitions:** Apply `.transition` to views entering and leaving.
2.  **Combine Transitions:** Compose transitions with `.combined(with:)`.
3.  **Asymmetric Transitions:** Use different insert and remove effects.
4.  **Animate Conditional Content:** Wrap conditional views so transitions trigger.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Transitions animate views as they appear and disappear, turning abrupt inserts and removes into smooth motion. SwiftUI ships transitions like `.opacity`, `.slide`, `.scale`, and `.move`, and you compose them (`.combined(with:)`) or make them asymmetric (one effect on insert, another on remove). The catch is that a transition only fires when the change is animated and the view's identity actually changes — so conditional content must be structured correctly. Well-chosen transitions guide the user's eye and make state changes feel coherent rather than jarring.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will animate views entering and leaving with composed, asymmetric transitions, smoothing every appearance and dismissal.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Transition:** An animation for a view's insertion or removal.
*   **Combined Transition:** Two transitions merged into one effect.
*   **Asymmetric Transition:** Different effects for insertion versus removal.
*   **View Identity:** The property change that triggers a transition to run.
