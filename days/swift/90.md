# Day 90: Navigation & Deep Linking - Handoff, Shortcuts & Continuity

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Support Handoff:** Advertise user activities with `NSUserActivity`.
2.  **Add App Shortcuts:** Expose actions as Siri/Spotlight shortcuts (preview of App Intents).
3.  **Restore from Activity:** Continue an activity on another device or from search.
4.  **Integrate with the System:** Make key screens reachable from outside the app.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Modern apps extend beyond their own window into the system. Handoff, via `NSUserActivity`, lets a user start a task on one device and continue it on another, and the same activities make your content searchable in Spotlight and continuable from search. App Shortcuts (which you'll deepen with App Intents next) surface your key actions to Siri and Spotlight. Restoring the right screen from an incoming activity reuses the programmatic-navigation skills you've built. This system integration is what makes an app feel like a first-class citizen of the Apple ecosystem rather than an island.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will integrate your app with Handoff, Spotlight, and shortcuts via user activities, making key screens reachable across the ecosystem.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Handoff:** Continuity feature to resume a task on another device.
*   **`NSUserActivity`:** An object describing a continuable/searchable user activity.
*   **App Shortcut:** An exposed action available via Siri/Spotlight.
*   **Continuity:** Apple's cross-device task continuation features.
