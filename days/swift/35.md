# Day 35: State & Data Flow - AppStorage & Scene Phase

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Persist Preferences:** Store simple settings with `@AppStorage` (UserDefaults-backed).
2.  **React to Scene Phase:** Observe `\.scenePhase` for active/inactive/background transitions.
3.  **Save on Background:** Persist state when the app moves to the background.
4.  **Combine with State:** Blend persisted preferences with in-memory `@State`.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Not all state lives only in memory. `@AppStorage` binds a property directly to `UserDefaults`, perfect for lightweight preferences like a theme choice or a toggle that should survive relaunches — a change updates both the UI and the stored value automatically. The `\.scenePhase` environment value reports when your app becomes active, inactive, or backgrounded, giving you the right moment to save work or pause activity. Together these let you build apps that remember user choices and behave correctly across the app lifecycle, without a full persistence layer.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will persist lightweight preferences and respond to app lifecycle transitions, making your app feel stateful and well-behaved across launches.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **`@AppStorage`:** A wrapper binding a property to a `UserDefaults` key.
*   **`UserDefaults`:** A store for small key-value preference data.
*   **Scene Phase:** The environment value reporting active/inactive/background state.
*   **App Lifecycle:** The states an app moves through during its run.
