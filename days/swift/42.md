# Day 42: User Input & Forms - Pickers, Toggles & Steppers

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Add a `Picker`:** Let users choose from options with different picker styles.
2.  **Use `Toggle` & `Stepper`:** Capture booleans and increment/decrement values.
3.  **Pick Dates:** Add a `DatePicker` with a range.
4.  **Choose Colors:** Add a `ColorPicker` where relevant.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Beyond text, SwiftUI ships a full set of input controls. `Picker` presents choices and adapts its appearance by style (menu, segmented, wheel); `Toggle` and `Stepper` handle booleans and numeric increments; `DatePicker` and `ColorPicker` cover dates and colors with native pickers. Each binds directly to your state, so the control and your model stay in sync automatically. Knowing the available controls — and their styles — lets you build rich forms without custom UI, keeping your app consistent with platform conventions.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will build rich input forms from SwiftUI's built-in controls, each bound directly to your state.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **`Picker`:** A control for selecting one option from several.
*   **`Toggle`/`Stepper`:** Controls for boolean and incremental numeric input.
*   **`DatePicker`:** A native control for choosing dates and times.
*   **Picker Style:** A modifier changing a picker's presentation (menu, segmented, wheel).
