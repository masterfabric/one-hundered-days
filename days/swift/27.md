# Day 27: Layout & Composition - ScrollView & Scroll APIs

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Scroll Content:** Wrap tall content in a `ScrollView` (vertical/horizontal).
2.  **Use `scrollTargetBehavior`:** Add paging/snapping with `.scrollTargetBehavior(.viewAligned)`.
3.  **Programmatic Scrolling:** Scroll to an item with `ScrollViewReader` and `scrollTo`.
4.  **Read Scroll Position:** Track offset with `scrollPosition` bindings.

## 📖 Task Explanations

`ScrollView` makes any content scrollable, and modern SwiftUI adds precise control over the experience. `scrollTargetBehavior` enables paging and snapping (great for carousels), `ScrollViewReader` lets you jump to a specific item programmatically, and scroll-position bindings let you observe or drive the current offset. Combined with lazy stacks and grids, these APIs give you smooth, performant scrolling over large data sets with the kind of polished behavior users expect from native apps.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will build polished scrolling experiences — paging, snapping, and programmatic scrolling — using SwiftUI's modern scroll APIs.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **`ScrollView`:** A container that makes its content scrollable.
*   **`scrollTargetBehavior`:** A modifier enabling snapping/paging on scroll.
*   **`ScrollViewReader`:** A proxy enabling programmatic scrolling to an anchor.
*   **Scroll Position:** A binding reflecting or controlling the current scroll offset.
