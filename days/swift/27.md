# Day 27: Layout & Composition - ScrollView & Scroll APIs

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Scroll Content:** Wrap tall content in a `ScrollView` (vertical/horizontal).
2.  **Use `scrollTargetBehavior`:** Add paging/snapping with `.scrollTargetBehavior(.viewAligned)`.
3.  **Programmatic Scrolling:** Scroll to an item with `ScrollViewReader` and `scrollTo`.
4.  **Read Scroll Position:** Track offset with `scrollPosition` bindings.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Wrap tall content in a `ScrollView` and it scrolls — vertically by default, horizontally with `ScrollView(.horizontal)`. Note that a `ScrollView` offers its content unlimited space along the scroll axis, which is why a child using `.frame(maxHeight: .infinity)` inside one behaves oddly; size the content, not the container.

Then add snapping. `.scrollTargetLayout()` on the inner stack plus `.scrollTargetBehavior(.viewAligned)` on the scroll view makes it settle on item boundaries instead of anywhere, which is the entire carousel experience in two modifiers. `.paging` snaps a full container width at a time, for onboarding-style screens.

Programmatic scrolling comes from `ScrollViewReader`. It hands you a proxy whose `scrollTo(_:anchor:)` jumps to any view carrying a matching `.id(...)` — useful for "scroll to the newest message" or returning to the top. Wrap the call in `withAnimation` if you want it to glide rather than teleport, and remember the target must actually exist in the hierarchy at that moment.

Finally, observe or drive the position with a `scrollPosition` binding, which reports the item currently scrolled to and lets you set it. This is how you sync a page indicator with a carousel, or restore a user's place when they return to a screen. Combined with lazy stacks and grids, these APIs give you smooth scrolling over large data sets with the polish users expect.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

By the end of today, you will build polished scrolling experiences — paging, snapping, and programmatic scrolling — using SwiftUI's modern scroll APIs.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **`ScrollView`:** A container that makes its content scrollable.
*   **`scrollTargetBehavior`:** A modifier enabling snapping/paging on scroll.
*   **`ScrollViewReader`:** A proxy enabling programmatic scrolling to an anchor.
*   **Scroll Position:** A binding reflecting or controlling the current scroll offset.
*   **`scrollTargetLayout`:** A modifier marking which layout's children are snap targets.
