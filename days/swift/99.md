# Day 99: Release & CI/CD - Continuous Delivery & Fastlane

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Automate Distribution:** Extend CI to upload builds to TestFlight automatically.
2.  **Use Fastlane:** Automate signing, building, and uploading with Fastlane lanes.
3.  **Manage Versioning:** Automate build-number bumps and changelogs.
4.  **Plan Phased Rollouts:** Release gradually and monitor before full rollout.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Continuous delivery automates getting a passing build into testers' or users' hands. Fastlane is the de-facto tool: its lanes script the tedious steps — managing certificates, building, and uploading to TestFlight or the App Store — so a release becomes one command (or an automatic CI step) instead of a manual ritual. Automating version bumps and changelog generation removes human error, and phased release rollouts let you ship to a small percentage first, watch metrics and crash rates, and expand only if healthy. This automation is what lets teams release frequently and confidently rather than dreading each launch.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will automate delivery to TestFlight and the App Store with Fastlane and phased rollouts, making releases routine and low-risk.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Continuous Delivery:** Automating the path from a passing build to distribution.
*   **Fastlane:** A tool automating iOS build, signing, and release steps.
*   **Lane:** A scripted sequence of Fastlane actions.
*   **Phased Rollout:** Releasing to a growing fraction of users while monitoring.
