# Day 25: SwiftUI Basics - Extracting Reusable Views

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Extract a Subview:** Pull a chunk of `body` into its own `View` struct.
2.  **Pass Data In:** Give the subview `let` properties for its inputs.
3.  **Keep Views Small:** Refactor a large body into focused, named components.
4.  **Preview Components in Isolation:** Add a `#Preview` for the extracted view alone.

## 📖 Task Explanations

As views grow, a single monolithic `body` becomes hard to read and slow to recompute. The remedy is extraction: move a self-contained piece into its own `View` struct that receives its data through simple `let` properties. Small, named components are easier to reason about, reuse across screens, preview in isolation, and — importantly — help SwiftUI update only what changed. This discipline of composing screens from many small views is one of the biggest differences between beginner and professional SwiftUI code.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will decompose large views into small, reusable components, improving readability, reuse, and rendering performance from day one.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Subview:** A view extracted into its own struct for reuse and clarity.
*   **Composition:** Building complex UI from many small views.
*   **Component:** A reusable, self-contained view with clear inputs.
*   **View Identity:** How SwiftUI tracks a view across updates to decide what to redraw.
