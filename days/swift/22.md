# Day 22: SwiftUI Basics - Stacks & Layout Primitives

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Arrange with `VStack`/`HStack`:** Stack views vertically and horizontally.
2.  **Overlay with `ZStack`:** Layer views back-to-front.
3.  **Control Spacing & Alignment:** Set `spacing:` and `alignment:` on stacks.
4.  **Push with `Spacer`:** Use `Spacer` and `padding` to distribute space.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Stacks are SwiftUI's fundamental layout containers, and the first two you need are `VStack` and `HStack`. A `VStack` arranges its children top-to-bottom, an `HStack` left-to-right, and both size themselves to fit what they contain. Put a few `Text` views in each and nest one inside the other — most real screens are nothing more than stacks inside stacks.

`ZStack` is the third primitive: it layers children back-to-front along the depth axis, with the first child at the back. This is how you place a caption over an image, put a badge on a corner, or lay a translucent scrim over content. Because layering is just another container, you compose it exactly like the other two.

Every stack takes an `alignment:` and a `spacing:` argument, and setting them deliberately is what separates a tidy layout from a cramped one. Alignment controls the cross axis — `.leading` in a `VStack`, `.top` in an `HStack` — and spacing sets the uniform gap between children. Pass `spacing: 0` when you want to control gaps yourself, and remember that the alignment guides of nested stacks are what keep columns of text lined up.

Finally, distribute the leftover space. `Spacer` is a view that expands to fill whatever room is available, so one `Spacer` pushes a view to an edge and two `Spacer`s centre it; `padding` adds breathing room around a view's own edges. Between alignment, spacing, spacers, and padding you can build almost any screen without a single manual frame calculation — SwiftUI negotiates sizes between parent and child for you, a model you'll deepen later.

By the end of today, you will compose real screen layouts from stacks, spacers, and alignment, the building blocks of every SwiftUI interface.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **`VStack`/`HStack`/`ZStack`:** Containers arranging children vertically, horizontally, or in depth.
*   **Alignment:** How a stack lines up its children on the cross axis.
*   **`Spacer`:** A flexible view that expands to push siblings apart.
*   **Padding:** Space added around a view's edges.
*   **Layout Container:** A view whose job is positioning its children rather than drawing.
*   **Size Negotiation:** SwiftUI's parent-proposes, child-decides process for assigning sizes.
