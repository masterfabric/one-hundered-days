# Day 64: Advanced SwiftUI UI - Liquid Glass

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Apply `glassEffect`:** Give a custom view a Liquid Glass material with `.glassEffect()`.
2.  **Tint & Shape Glass:** Configure the glass tint and clip shape.
3.  **Group Glass Elements:** Combine multiple glass views coherently.
4.  **Adopt System Glass:** Rely on standard components getting Liquid Glass automatically.

## 📖 Task Explanations

iOS 26 introduced Liquid Glass, Apple's adaptive material with glass-like refraction, reflection, and fluid motion. Standard SwiftUI components adopt it automatically, so much of the look comes for free. For custom UI, the composable `glassEffect()` modifier integrates your own views into the system's material language — you can tint it, clip it to a shape, and group related glass elements so they read as one surface. Using Liquid Glass deliberately (and sparingly) makes custom components feel native and current rather than flat and dated.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will integrate custom components into iOS 26's Liquid Glass material system with `glassEffect`, giving your UI a native, current feel.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Liquid Glass:** iOS 26's adaptive, glass-like material system.
*   **`glassEffect`:** The composable SwiftUI modifier applying Liquid Glass.
*   **Material:** A translucent, depth-aware surface style.
*   **Glass Grouping:** Combining glass elements so they read as one surface.
