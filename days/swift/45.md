# Day 45: User Input & Forms - Gestures

## 📝 Today's Tasks

1.  **Add Tap & Long-Press:** Attach `.onTapGesture` and `.onLongPressGesture`.
2.  **Drag Views:** Track movement with `DragGesture` and update offset state.
3.  **Compose Gestures:** Combine gestures with `.simultaneously`, `.sequenced`, `.exclusively`.
4.  **Animate Gesture State:** Use `@GestureState` for transient interaction state.

## 📖 Task Explanations

Gestures make interfaces feel alive. Beyond taps and long-presses, `DragGesture`, `MagnifyGesture`, and `RotateGesture` capture continuous interaction, and you compose them (simultaneous, sequenced, exclusive) to build complex behaviors like drag-to-dismiss or pinch-to-zoom. `@GestureState` holds transient state that automatically resets when a gesture ends, which is perfect for in-progress drags. Pairing gestures with animation (next section) produces the fluid, direct-manipulation feel that defines great iOS apps.

## 💡 What You'll Gain

You will add rich, composable gesture interactions and manage transient gesture state, the basis for fluid direct-manipulation UI.

## 📚 Dictionary for Developers

*   **Gesture:** A recognizer for user touch interactions.
*   **`DragGesture`:** A gesture tracking continuous movement.
*   **Gesture Composition:** Combining gestures simultaneously, sequentially, or exclusively.
*   **`@GestureState`:** State tied to an in-progress gesture that auto-resets.
